Good to know

Frequently asked.

Do I pay before I see anything?+
Never. You upload a photo, we create your portrait, and we email you a free digital proof. You only pay once you've seen it and you love it.
What kind of photo works best?+
One clear, reasonably well-lit photo where your pet's face is visible. A relaxed phone photo is perfect — close-ups and good light beat professional gear every time.
How long does it take?+
Your first proof lands within 24–48 hours. Digital files are delivered the moment you approve; printed and framed pieces are made on demand and typically ship within 3–6 business days.
What sizes and materials can I get?+
Digital files, premium posters, gallery canvas, and framed prints (natural wood, black or white) — from 8×10″ up to 24×30″. Everything is printed with museum-grade inks and stock.
Can you do more than one pet?+
Absolutely. Mention it when you order (or just reply to your proof email) and we'll arrange a multi-pet portrait.
What if I don't love it?+
We'll refine it until you do — and if we still can't get it right, you owe nothing. That's the proof-first promise.
