Student-led out-and-back trail fundraiser

Run for donuts. Give for SLO.

Donut Dash is a community run and canned food drive benefiting SLO Food Bank, starting and finishing at the Avila Beach Drive entrance to the Bob Jones Trail.

Only paid runners receive an official number, name sticker, and timed entry. Prepaid runners get pre-made check-in stickers; race-day cash entries can be printed on site.

All proceeds from this fundraiser go directly to SLO Food Bank.

Event snapshot

Out-and-back run on the Bob Jones City-to-the-Sea Trail. Roughly 3 miles on a flat, paved path — open to all ages and paces. Canned food collection, race-day check-in, and donuts at the finish.

Event details

🍩 Cause

Benefiting SLO Food Bank through cash donations and canned food collection. All proceeds go directly to SLO Food Bank.

🏃 Distance

Out-and-back along the Bob Jones Trail from the Avila Beach Drive entrance — roughly 3 miles total at your own pace.

🏁 Finish line

Every runner finishes at the Avila Beach Drive entrance, then walks to Avila Beach Community Park (pirate ship playground) to celebrate with donuts.

📋 Check-in

Paid runners check in near the trail entrance and receive a name sticker and official timing placement.

Race-day flow

1

Check in

Arrive at the Bob Jones Trail entrance off Avila Beach Drive, find your name, confirm waiver status, and pick up your race sticker packet.

2

Donate food

Drop canned goods at the collection table before you head out onto the trail.

3

Run your race

Follow the Bob Jones Trail from the Avila Beach Drive entrance, run 1.5 miles out to the marked turnaround, then head 1.5 miles back to the finish.

4

Celebrate

After you finish, walk to the pirate ship park at Avila Beach Community Park for donuts, photos, and the final fundraising total.

Course map

Volunteer roles

We need a small crew to make the event run smoothly. Volunteering is a great way to support the cause without registering as a runner.

Check-in crew

Name lookup, waiver confirmation, and sticker packet handoff at the entrance tables.

Course marshals

Direct runners at key turns, keep the turnaround clear, and cheer people on!

Food + finish team

Collect cans, count totals, and hand out donuts at the pirate ship park.

Sponsorship

Local businesses and community partners can support Donut Dash — help us raise more for SLO Food Bank by sponsoring event costs, providing refreshments, or contributing directly to the fundraiser.

🥇 Title sponsor

Logo at the top of the website, on event signage, and in race-day announcements. Ideal for a business that wants to be the main presenting supporter.

🥈 Supporting sponsor

Logo on the site, volunteer shirts, or finish-line signage, plus a shout-out at the start and finish of the race.

🍩 In-kind sponsor

Provide donuts, coffee, water, or supplies. In-kind sponsors are listed on the site and thanked at the event.

Interested in sponsoring Donut Dash? Email donutdash2026@gmail.com to talk about options that work for your business.
