How do I download my CSV from Chase & PayPal? ▾
Chase:
  1. Sign in at chase.com → pick the account.
  2. Click the download icon near the activity list.
  3. Choose "Spreadsheet (CSV)", pick a date range, and download.
PayPal:
  1. Sign in → go to Activity → Statements → Download.
  2. Pick a date range, file type CSV, and download.

Then drag both files onto this page (or use "Choose CSV files"). The dashboard auto-detects which is which.
